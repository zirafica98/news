Questions

Do I need to do anything every day?

No. You get a short report every day. If you want, posts can wait for your approval before they go live.

Which networks?

Instagram (posts, Reels, stories), YouTube (Shorts and long videos), Threads and Telegram. TikTok is possible with a quick manual step.

Does it post on my accounts?

Yes, through the official Instagram, YouTube, Threads and Telegram APIs. You keep full ownership and can revoke access any time.

Which languages?

English, Serbian and most major languages, for both text and voice.

Is the content copied from the sources?

No. Stories are summarized in your own words, and we always respect the platforms' rules.
