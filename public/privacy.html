Privacy Policy

AI Morning Brief · Last updated October 24, 2026

AI Morning Brief is a daily AI news briefing published on this website and on social media (Instagram, Threads, YouTube and Telegram as @ai.morning.brief / AI Morning Brief). This policy explains what data is processed.

Website visitors

  • Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors.
  • Push notifications. If you turn on notifications, your browser's push subscription (an anonymous endpoint and keys) is stored so we can send the daily edition. You can turn it off at any time in the site settings or your browser.
  • Ratings. Thumbs up / down on items are stored anonymously, without any personal information, and only used to improve future editions.
  • Saved items and settings stay in your browser (local storage) and are never sent to us.

Google and YouTube data

AI Morning Brief uses the YouTube Data API only to upload its own daily video to its own YouTube channel. The app is used only by the channel owner. It accesses the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos and to read the list of the channel's own uploads (to avoid posting the same video twice). It does not access, collect or store data of any other YouTube or Google users. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.

Use of YouTube data is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.

Social media

Posts are published through the official Instagram, Threads, YouTube and Telegram APIs. We do not collect data about people who view or interact with those posts beyond the aggregate statistics the platforms show to every account owner.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @ai.morning.brief on Instagram.
